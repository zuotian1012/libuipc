# Contact bottleneck probe (2026-09-16)

This is preliminary research evidence, not an accepted optimization. The full protocol, environment, raw logs, and limitations are in [`experiments/2026-09-16-preliminary.md`](../../experiments/2026-09-16-preliminary.md) and [`experiments/env.md`](../../experiments/env.md).

The in-repository `94_contact_density_probe` generated parallel cloth stacks on an RTX 3060 Ti. All 36 fresh-process, 40-frame throughput runs completed, converged, and passed ground and vertical-slab separation checks in the contact conditions. For a four-layer, 16x16-grid scene, the median frame time over frames 6–40 was 20.160 ms with contact and 5.369 ms for the same cloth mesh with contact disabled. The ratio was 3.76x; the contact run had fewer Newton iterations, so increased Newton count does not explain this case's difference.

A separate synchronized timing run attributed 205.5 ms of its 971.1 ms pipeline total to DCD detection and 121.6 ms to trajectory-candidate detection over 40 frames. Timer synchronization changes wall time, so those figures describe stage attribution only. In canonical active-pair diagnostics, settled frames 33–40 had median across-frame `R_new = 0.078%` in the four-layer 16-grid variant. During contact formation, median within-frame `R_new` was 21.5% across 13 nonempty comparisons. Three other grid/layer variants showed similarly low settled across-frame churn, but they remain one scene family.

The follow-up in [`experiments/next_step/2026-09-16-followup.md`](../../experiments/next_step/2026-09-16-followup.md) classified each DCD pass in six full-baseline runs and measured its core time. One of 246 passes with active simplex pairs found no new pair; the other zero-new passes occurred before active contact. The optimistic whole-call zero-new time removal was only 3.33–3.92% of synchronized pipeline time in the second batch, below the 1.2x gate. The global all-or-nothing refresh idea is stopped for this scene. Regional validity remains unmeasured.

Opt-in `UIPC_PROBE_TOI_HIST` now records per-type TOI histograms after unchanged CCD kernels, including the ground vertex–plane filter. Frame 18 had simplex TOIs below one despite the frame-stat `last_ccd_toi` being one. A separate strict-mode speed sweep captured ground TOIs below one at initial speeds of 1–8 m/s. Per-query CCD iteration cost and general collision correctness remain to be evaluated before any production optimization.

In an additional 20-frame four-layer impact comparison (0 versus 2 m/s initial descent), timer-free median frame time increased from 17.60 to 21.58 ms. In separate synchronized stage runs without TOI histogram downloads, `Filter CCD TOI` time was roughly unchanged at 12.91 versus 13.06 ms over 20 frames. Contact begins earlier in the faster run, so the comparison does not isolate per-query CCD cost. It does not support prioritizing CCD TOI kernel optimization in this scene.
